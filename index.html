How It Works

WhatsApp: Click the WhatsApp icon on any date to send a message with that date's information.

Calendar: Click the calendar icon to create an event for that date in Google Calendar.

Message Format: "Today is the (date) of this (month), (year). It's a (day of the week). Today is (Tamil month) (Valar/Thei) Pirai (event type)."
